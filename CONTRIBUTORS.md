## Tools and assistance

This project was built with help from **Claude Code** (Anthropic's AI coding assistant), directed and reviewed by the author. Claude Code was used to:

- Write and refine the HTML, CSS and JavaScript, including the course planner, the "On this page" navigation and the FAQ controls
- Apply two design-review skills, *Impeccable* and *Taste*, to remove generic "AI-looking" patterns and polish layout, type and motion
- Redesign the hero section around a real WashU photo
- Fact-check every program number and policy against official WashU and U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics pages (November 24, 2026), and correct the errors it found
- Test the page on simulated phones and tablets with automated touch tests, and fix the layout and tap-size problems they found

All design choices, final content and the decision to publish were made by the author.
